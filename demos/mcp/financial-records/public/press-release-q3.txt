ACME CORP REPORTS STRONG Q3 2025 RESULTS

SAN FRANCISCO — Acme Corp (NASDAQ: ACME) today reported financial results
for the third quarter ended September 30, 2025.

Q3 2025 HIGHLIGHTS:
- Revenue: $612 million (up 17% YoY)
- Subscription Revenue: $485 million (up 24% YoY)
- Customer Count: 2,400+ enterprise customers
- Net Retention Rate: 135%

"Our Q3 results demonstrate the growing demand for AI-native security
infrastructure," said CEO Jane Smith. "The launch of our Agent Authorization
platform has been met with extraordinary customer interest, with 50+
enterprises already in pilot programs."
